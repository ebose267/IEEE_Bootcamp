The Bootcamp

The problem isn't the research.

It's that nobody teaches the writing.

Across Nigerian engineering faculties, the thesis and research paper is the single biggest academic bottleneck students face — yet almost no formal training exists in how to actually write one. Students learn by trial and error, under deadline pressure, with inconsistent supervision and low publication output.

IEEE UNIBEN has built a complete, ready-to-run 2-week Research Writing Bootcamp for 100–300 Level students — a structured, framework-driven curriculum that takes a student from a raw topic to a finished, defensible research proposal across 10 live sessions, delivered through daily mentor-led pods of 10.

It's opened as a multi-branch partnership: other IEEE Student Branches and student organisations can co-host a cohort, nominate students, or provide pod mentors — turning a single-branch idea into a cross-branch collaboration.
