01
The Bootcamp

The problem isn't the research.
It's that nobody teaches the writing.

Across Nigerian engineering faculties, the thesis and research paper is the single biggest academic bottleneck students face — yet almost no formal training exists in how to actually write one. Students learn by trial and error, under deadline pressure, with inconsistent supervision and low publication output.

IEEE UNIBEN has built a complete, ready-to-run 2-week Research Writing Bootcamp for 100–300 Level students — a structured, framework-driven curriculum that takes a student from a raw topic to a finished, defensible research proposal across 10 live sessions, delivered through daily mentor-led pods of 10.

It's opened as a multi-branch partnership: other IEEE Student Branches and student organisations can co-host a cohort, nominate students, or provide pod mentors — turning a single-branch idea into a cross-branch collaboration.

The 10-day build

Day 01

Why Research Matters & the Research Mindset

Spot credible vs. weak sources and see why research skills matter beyond coursework.

Day 02

How to Read a Paper Fast

The three-pass method — summarize a paper's core contribution in under 20 minutes.

Day 03

Identifying Research Gaps & Problem Statements

Turn a genuine gap into a clear, testable problem statement, scoped for undergraduate work.

Day 04

Research Methodology Basics for Engineers

Justify a chosen methodology — experimental, simulation, survey, or comparative — for your problem.

Day 05

Citation, IEEE Referencing & Research Ethics

Format IEEE references correctly and identify plagiarism in a sample text.

Day 06

Anatomy of a Paper (IMRaD) & Abstract Writing

Draft a structured 150–250 word abstract that communicates problem, method, and contribution.

Day 07

Writing the Introduction & Literature Review

Draft an introduction that moves logically from context to your own contribution.

Day 08

Methods, Results & Data Visualization · 2 hrs

Draft a methods section and produce one clear, correctly labeled figure or table.

Day 09

Peer Review: How Reviewers Think · 2 hrs

Write a structured, constructive peer review of a classmate's draft using a shared rubric.

Day 10

Submission, Publishing & Capstone

Submit a polished, submission-ready mini-paper/proposal incorporating your peer's feedback.

Certificate criteria: 8/10 sessions attended · 8/10 assignments submitted · Day 9 peer review + Day 10 capstone completed.
